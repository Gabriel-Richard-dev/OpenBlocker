# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We, as members, contributors and maintainers, pledge to make participation in
the OpenBlock community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Respecting different opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Taking responsibility for our mistakes and learning from them.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and for the people who use
  OpenBlock.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

OpenBlock is about helping people with their own intentions. Shaming people for
how they use their phones has no place in issues, discussions or the product.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and
may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other
contributions that do not align with this code of conduct.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through
<https://github.com/Gabriel-Richard-dev/OpenBlocker/security/advisories/new>
(choose "Report a vulnerability" and mention that it is a conduct report) or by
contacting a maintainer directly. All reports will be reviewed promptly and
handled confidentially.

Maintainers will follow these steps: a private, written warning for a first
minor issue; a temporary ban for serious or repeated issues; a permanent ban for
a sustained pattern of violations or harassment.
